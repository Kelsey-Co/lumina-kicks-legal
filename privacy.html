Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Lumina Kicks ("the App") is a personal pregnancy movement tracking application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

2. Data Collection

Lumina Kicks does not collect, store, or transmit your baby movement records, pregnancy records, or optional profile information. All movement records and optional profile information entered for PDF exports are stored exclusively on your device using local storage (SQLite).

For purchase and premium feature management, Google Play Billing and RevenueCat may process anonymous device identifiers and purchase-related information. No movement records, pregnancy data, or profile information are shared with these services.

3. Network and Third-Party Services

Movement records and profile information remain on your device and are not transmitted to us.

The App uses Google Play Billing and RevenueCat solely to process purchases, restore purchases, verify premium access, and manage entitlement status. These services may process anonymous device identifiers and purchase-related information necessary for app functionality.

The App may use Firebase Analytics to understand general app usage and help improve the user experience. Firebase Analytics may collect app interaction data and device-related identifiers. We do not use advertising networks or sell personal data.

4. Data Storage

All movement data is stored locally on your device. You have full control over your data at all times. You can delete all data from within the App via Settings → Delete All Data.

5. Data Deletion

If you delete the App or use the "Delete All Data" function, your locally stored App data is permanently removed and cannot be recovered by us.

6. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Privacy Rights

Because movement records and profile information are stored locally on your device and are not collected by us, we cannot access, correct, or delete that information for you. You can manage or delete your local App data directly in the App.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected within the App and on this page with an updated effective date.
